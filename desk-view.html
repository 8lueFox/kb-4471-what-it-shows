How to tell them apart

  • Parser (src/lib/kb/causes.ts, causeOf): the shared parser reads every date month-first. A day-first tenant's dates come out with month and day flipped. Any channel, any day-first tenant. This is the bug #4471 was opened for, and the queued fix is for it.
  • Hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type what the hint tells them — month first — and the form then reads it the tenant's way, day-first. Same-looking symptom, different bug: only channel form, only booking-v3 tenants (alder.physio de-DE, copper.studio es-ES, pale.harbour pt-PT).
The hint bug will not be fixed by the queued parser fix. Until it is, those tenants' form reports will keep arriving — and keep reading as “known”.

Why the desk saw one problem

  • Intake tags disagree with the real cause in 38 of 412 reports: 23 tagged hint are parser, 15 tagged parser are hint. Tag, not tenant+form, drove the merge queue.
  • Decision 0001 merges reports on subject lines alone. All of these subjects say the same thing (“wrong day”, “dates mixed up”), so two bugs merged into one ticket. The decision accepted this (“engineering merges them later”) — this page is that merge, done.

What to do

Re-open #4471 into the two tickets above. The parser fix is already queued; the hint fix is not, and every day it waits, the desk files its reports under a bug that cannot explain them.
