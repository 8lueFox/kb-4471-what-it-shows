How to tell them apart

  • Parser (src/lib/kb/causes.ts, causeOf): the shared parser reads every date month-first. A day-first tenant's dates come out with month and day flipped. Any channel, any day-first tenant. This is the bug #4471 was opened for.
  • Hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, people type what the hint tells them — month first — and the form then reads it the tenant's way, day-first. Same-looking symptom, different bug: only channel form, only booking-v3 tenants (alder.physio de-DE, copper.studio es-ES, pale.harbour pt-PT).
  • One-day move: the date moves by one day, or lands on a different weekday across time zones. Bodies say “Tuesday vs Monday”, “only June”, “Lisbon Tuesday vs Toronto Monday”, “invite arrived before the booking”. No month/day swap anywhere in the text.

Why the desk saw one problem

  • Intake tags disagree with the real cause in 38 of 412 reports: 23 tagged hint are parser, 15 tagged parser are hint. Tag, not tenant+form, drove the merge queue.
  • Decision 0001 merges reports on subject lines alone. All of these subjects say the same thing (“wrong day”, “dates mixed up”), so three bugs merged into one ticket. The decision accepted this (“engineering merges them later”) — this page is that merge, done.

What to do

Re-open #4471 into the three tickets above. Until the parser fix ships, the hint bug will keep producing reports the desk reads as “known” — it is not, and it will not be fixed by the queued parser fix.
